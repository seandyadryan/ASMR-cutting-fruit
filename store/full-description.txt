A little slice of quiet.

Step into a warm, unhurried kitchen with ASMR Cutting Fruit. Swipe across fresh fruit and vegetables, watch the pieces gently separate, and enjoy crafted cutting sounds that respond to every satisfying slice.

There is no score to chase in Free Slice. Pick an ingredient, find your rhythm, and make a small moment for yourself.

FRESH TEXTURES, SIMPLE PLEASURES
Explore 12 ingredients: watermelon, kiwi, orange, apple, strawberry, dragon fruit, cucumber, carrot, tomato, avocado, lemon and eggplant. Discover juicy, soft and crisp sound textures as you slice. Every ingredient is available from the start.

THREE WAYS TO MAKE A MOMENT
• Free Slice: enjoy the cutting board at your own pace, without a timer.
• Focus Session: take a gentle, one-minute slicing break.
• Daily Basket: finish six cuts on each of five ingredients, with a fresh selection each day.

MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME
Choose a maple, walnut or sage cutting board. Adjust the sound volume, add a soft kitchen ambience, switch gentle haptics on or off, or reduce motion. Headphones bring out the little details.

YOUR LITTLE RITUAL
Visit your Journal to see the slices you have made, the textures you have tried and the daily baskets you have completed. Progress and preferences stay on your device.

OFFLINE BY NATURE
Play without an internet connection. No ads, accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases. No energy meters or locked ingredients. Just your next slice.

Made for phones and larger screens, with a warm visual style, native Android controls and an adaptive cutting-board layout.

Take your time. The kitchen is yours.
