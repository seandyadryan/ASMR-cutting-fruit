Privacy Policy · Effective November 8, 2026
Developer: PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti
Android package: com.deploydulupulangnanti.asmrcuttingfruit

The short version
ASMR Cutting Fruit works offline. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your total slice count, ingredients you have tried, dates of completed daily baskets, and preferences for sounds, volume, ambience, haptics, reduced motion and board appearance. These records are kept in the app's private storage on your Android device. They are not sent to the developer or a third party. Android cloud backup for the app is disabled.

Permissions and device features

The app requests the normal Android vibration permission to provide optional haptic feedback when slicing. You can disable haptics in the app settings. The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, advertising identifier or shared storage access. Sounds and images are bundled with the app.

No third-party tracking or purchases

This version contains no advertising networks, analytics, third-party sign-in, crash-reporting services or in-app purchases. The developer receives no usage logs from the app. Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies; the developer does not control those services.

When you contact support

The Contact support button opens your chosen email application. If you choose to send an email, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you provide. We use that information only to respond to and resolve your support request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is retained only while needed for the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us. Your email provider processes your message under its own policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can change audio, haptic and appearance settings inside the app. To erase all locally stored progress and preferences, open Android Settings → Apps → ASMR Cutting Fruit → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. There is no online account or developer-hosted gameplay database to delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players. There are no chat, account or social sharing features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release information.
